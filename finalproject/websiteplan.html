Website Plan

HVAC Design Collaboration

Site Name

HVAC Design Collaboration

This name was selected because it clearly represents the purpose of the website: promoting collaboration between architects and HVAC specialists during building design.

Site Purpose

The website will educate architects, engineering students, construction professionals, and building owners about the importance of involving HVAC specialists early in the building design process. It will provide information on energy efficiency, indoor air quality, cost savings, system performance, and best practices for collaboration.

Scenarios

  • Why should an architect consult an HVAC engineer before completing a building layout?
  • What problems can occur when HVAC requirements are not considered during the design stage?
  • How does early HVAC coordination improve energy efficiency and occupant comfort?

Best Practices for HVAC Coordination

  • Engage HVAC engineers during conceptual design.
  • Allocate sufficient ceiling space for ductwork.
  • Use BIM coordination to avoid clashes.
  • Provide maintenance access to equipment.
  • Consider future expansion requirements.
